Direct connections between subjects

Pairs of loaded subjects whose numbers appear in each other's logs — they are talking directly. Strongest evidence of conspiracy.

Mutual contacts — network bridges

Contacts that appear in 2+ subjects' logs. These are the highest-value next-warrant candidates because surveilling them gives you visibility into multiple existing subjects' networks at once. Already-loaded subjects are excluded from this list.
